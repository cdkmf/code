QUICK ANSWERS

know what to expect.

Need clarity on pickup timing, pricing or service coverage? Here are the questions we hear most often.

Request a written quotation ↗
Can you arrange same-day pickup?

You can request same-day pickup. Our team checks your pickup PIN code, request time, carrier availability and shipment readiness before confirming whether pickup is possible that day.

Which locations do you serve?

We coordinate courier and freight movements across India through multiple carrier options. Serviceability, pickup coverage and timelines depend on your exact origin and destination PIN codes.

How do I get a freight rate?

Share the pickup and delivery PIN codes, chargeable or approximate weight, package count, dimensions, commodity and preferred mode. We'll discuss the applicable route and commercial terms.

Do you support regular corporate shipments?

Yes. Discuss monthly shipment volume, common routes and service requirements to explore account-specific rates, scheduled pickups, MIS and billing arrangements.

Can I track my shipment?

Use the carrier-tracking section for its official website. You can also contact our team for coordination and follow-ups using your AWB or docket number.
